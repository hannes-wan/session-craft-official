# Session Craft

**Break the record open. Rehearse the part that matters.**

Session Craft turns local audio into a focused practice session: separate the mix, follow the waveform, loop hard sections, shift speed or pitch, read the chord timeline, and export a backing track when the session is ready.

## The problem it solves

**Practice should start with the song, not a cloud upload or DAW project.**

Built for players and teachers who practice from real recordings, not sterile exercises. Session Craft keeps the song local, the controls immediate, and the rehearsal loop sharp enough for daily work.

## Why you should try it

- You practice with local recordings, demos, rehearsals, or private tracks that should not be uploaded just to loop a hard section.
- You want stems, loops, speed, pitch, chords, and export without opening a full DAW.
- You need a repeatable practice session, not a generic media player.

## What makes it strong

### The source stays yours

- Import MP3, WAV, and FLAC files and build the session around the recording you actually need to learn.
- The workflow is local by design: local files, local projects, private rehearsal material, and no upload-first practice loop.
- Guitar, bass, keys, vocals, and teachers all stay close to the original recording while changing loop, chord, pitch, speed, and mix decisions.

### The mix becomes playable

- Professional stem separation creates practical controls for vocals, drums, bass, guitar, and other material when the paid workflow is enabled.
- Solo the part you need to study, lower the part you want to replace, or keep drums and bass in place for timing.
- The goal is not a laboratory remix. The goal is a practice mix that makes the next ten minutes more useful.

### Repetition stays musical

- A/B loops, speed control, pitch shift, BPM detection, and chord regions live on the same timeline, so repetition does not lose musical context.
- Slow a hard phrase, transpose for the instrument, inspect chord movement, and bring tempo back only when the part is under control.
- Project save and backing-track export turn the session into reusable practice material instead of a one-off playback tweak.

### $19 lifetime for a rehearsal engine

- Community covers local song import and basic practice state; Professional unlocks stem separation, speed, pitch, chords, loops, projects, and export.
- No subscription is needed for the desktop workflow, and no cloud account is required to practice a private rehearsal file.
- Buy it when one saved session is worth more than juggling separate stem, slowdown, chord, loop, and export tools.

## What you can do with it

- **Local audio import** — Open MP3, WAV, and FLAC files and turn the source recording into a private, repeatable desktop session.
- **Stem-focused practice** — Separate vocals, drums, bass, guitar, and other material when the paid stem workflow is enabled, then solo, mute, or reshape the practice mix.
- **Loop, tempo, pitch, and chords** — Set A/B loops on the waveform, slow a passage without losing pitch, transpose, detect BPM, and keep chord regions under the playhead.
